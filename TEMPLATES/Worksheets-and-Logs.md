# Reusable Worksheets and Logs

**Purpose:** Collect, in one place, blank versions of every reusable form used in this library, each linked to a filled example in the chapter that teaches it. Copy a template into your project; keep the filled example open beside it as a guide.

**Note:** blank cells below are intentional — these are templates. Every template has a filled example elsewhere in the library (or, for the two forms not used elsewhere, directly below the template).
