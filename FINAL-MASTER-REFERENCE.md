# Final Master Reference

**Purpose:** A concise reference to the whole library — the principles, first questions, default moves, traps and checks for each domain — with links to the canonical chapters. It is a reminder, not a replacement: every line here is explained, qualified and exemplified in the owner document.

---

## Ten principles that run through everything

1. **Name the job before the tool.** Every decision answers what the viewer should notice, understand or feel. ([Decision Method](FOUNDATIONS/Decision-Method.md))
2. **Hold two live options, including doing nothing.** ([Decision Method](FOUNDATIONS/Decision-Method.md#21-using-the-pattern-in-practice))
3. **Decide how you will verify before you change anything.** ([Decision Method](FOUNDATIONS/Decision-Method.md))
4. **Label confidence honestly** — requirement, practice, heuristic, preference, illustration. ([Decision Method](FOUNDATIONS/Decision-Method.md#4-confidence-labels))
5. **Truth outranks polish in factual work.** No stitched testimony, misleading order, invented evidence or undisclosed alteration. ([Performance Editing](EDITING/Performance-Editing.md#interview-compression-what-is-permissible), [Rights, Consent and Disclosure](OPERATIONS/Rights-Consent-and-Disclosure.md))
6. **Solve upstream first.** Story and performance before effects; capture before repair. ([Failure Modes and Repair](DIAGNOSTICS/Failure-Modes-and-Repair.md#1-diagnose-before-you-treat))
7. **Pace is the rate of meaningful change, not the cut rate.** ([Rhythm and Pacing](EDITING/Rhythm-and-Pacing.md#1-pace-is-not-cut-rate))
8. **No single number fits every destination** — loudness, caption rates, dimensions. ([Audio Delivery](DELIVERY/Audio-Delivery.md), [Captions and Accessibility](TEXT-ACCESSIBILITY/Captions-and-Accessibility.md))
9. **Versions are new edits, not resizes.** ([Platform and Format Strategy](DELIVERY/Platform-and-Format-Strategy.md))
10. **Coherence is observable:** every choice explained by the same intent; every exception has a reason. ([Creative Coherence and Taste](FOUNDATIONS/Creative-Coherence-and-Taste.md))

## Foundations

**Brief.** Turn adjectives into a testable one-sentence intent; separate business, story, viewer, accessibility and delivery layers; find hinge variables; move commercial asks into the organisation's own voice rather than bending testimony. *Trap:* success defined as a style or vanity metric. *Check:* a fresh viewer can be tested against the intent. → [Creative Brief](FOUNDATIONS/Creative-Brief.md)

**Constraints.** Spend on the part that decides success; write a priority map with consequences; run late changes through a dependency-ordered change process. *Trap:* spreading scarcity evenly. → [Constraints and Priorities](FOUNDATIONS/Constraints-and-Priorities.md)

**References.** Analyse function (information order, attention, contrast, density, sound, payoff), translate rather than copy; label what not to take. *Trap:* temp love; borrowing another genre's surface. → [Reference Analysis](FOUNDATIONS/Reference-Analysis.md)

## Story

**Interview.** Design the conversation from the viewer contract; choose the subject for direct experience, not fluency; chronology before interpretation; sensitive topics late or pre-agreed; patient silence; log live; never feed words. *Check:* would the subject recognise the edited statement as theirs? → [Interview Craft](STORY/Interview-Craft.md)

**Structure.** Track what the viewer knows, expects, wants and feels; choose the dominant form (arc, argument, demonstration, montage, conversation); keep an open-question ledger; restructure without false causation. → [Story Architecture](STORY/Story-Architecture.md)

**Contracts.** Genre sets truth status, orientation, evidence burden and pace; retention tactics are not universal; bend contracts openly. → [Genre and Viewer Contract](STORY/Genre-and-Viewer-Contract.md)

## Operations

**Assessment.** Technical sweep first; watch at full speed; rate truth and technique separately; link selects to context; know what cannot be recovered. → [Footage Assessment](OPERATIONS/Footage-Assessment.md)

**Ingest.** Two checksum-verified copies; one documented clock (rate, DF/NDF); conform VFR; check sync at three points; never deliver from proxies. → [Ingest and Preparation](OPERATIONS/Ingest-and-Preparation.md)

**Assembly.** Plan the cut on transcripts before the timeline: mark lines verbatim with source timecodes, order them against the beat map, read the paper edit aloud to a fresh listener, then build the radio cut — sound in story order — and layer picture over it; check every join *and* split against the full answer. → [Paper Edit and Assembly](OPERATIONS/Paper-Edit-and-Assembly.md)

**Handoffs.** Lock is a process; turnovers with handles and references; no interchange preserves every effect; test restore. → [Production and Handoffs](OPERATIONS/Production-and-Handoffs.md)

**Reviews.** Notes are symptoms; categorise (technical, factual, accessibility, taste, scope, legal, ethical); resolve conflicts by objective and owner; name, offer, escalate when a change would mislead; where agreed, the subject reviews their own sections, and meaning and dignity outrank every other round. → [Review and Revisions](OPERATIONS/Review-and-Revisions.md)

**Rights.** Permission must exist and be evidenced; consent is informed and specific and continues after the form is signed — subjects see their sections where agreed and are cared for after difficult material; licences cover actual use; disclose reconstruction and realistic alteration; mask robustly. → [Rights, Consent and Disclosure](OPERATIONS/Rights-Consent-and-Disclosure.md)

## Editing

**The cut.** Cut when the thought completes, not only the motion; weigh intention, breath, look, gesture, sound, suspense, saturation; compare candidates by A/B at full speed from well before the cut; break rules with a stated undo condition. → [Cut Decisions](EDITING/Cut-Decisions.md)

**Shot relationships.** Choose continuity, contrast or collision per cut; check every relationship variable for accidental collisions; split edits are tools, not upgrades. → [Shot Relationships](EDITING/Shot-Relationships.md)

**Pacing.** Diagnose which component (density, energy, movement, sound, emotion) is "slow"; vary tempo; protect comprehension pauses and landings; beware your own habituation. → [Rhythm and Pacing](EDITING/Rhythm-and-Pacing.md)

**Performance.** Build from the best-arc take; reactions are portable in fiction, not in documentary; keep qualifications that carry meaning. → [Performance Editing](EDITING/Performance-Editing.md)

**Continuity and attention.** Protect the viewer's map; reset geography with neutral shots or motivated crossings; infer eye trace from faces, motion, brightness, focus, text; reframing changes meaning. → [Continuity and Attention](EDITING/Continuity-and-Attention.md)

## Motion design and text

**Design.** Start from the graphic's job; design resting frames as stills; animatic before build; define what moves, what stays, where the eye lands; build a system versions inherit. → [Design System](MOTION-DESIGN/Design-System.md)

**Animation.** Timing and spacing; read velocity curves; linear motion is right for constant-rate systems and data sweeps; overshoot suits play, not finance or medicine; animate only what carries meaning. → [Animation Grammar](MOTION-DESIGN/Animation-Grammar.md)

**Integration and data.** Decide overlay, anchored overlay or presence; match blur, grain, colour; bars from zero, units, sources, no overshoot on values, no implied causation. → [Integration and Data Graphics](MOTION-DESIGN/Integration-and-Data-Graphics.md)

**Typography.** Separate text by function; test sizes on the smallest device over worst-case backgrounds; shorten wording before shrinking type; never letter-space Devanagari or split grapheme clusters. → [Typography System](TEXT-ACCESSIBILITY/Typography-System.md)

**Captions.** Time to speech; chunk by syntax; reading rates are destination- and language-specific; snap to cuts; move for faces and action; never fabricate inaudible words; verify on encoded files. → [Captions and Accessibility](TEXT-ACCESSIBILITY/Captions-and-Accessibility.md)

**Localization.** Translate meaning, adapt copy, transliterate consistently; brief translators on gender and context; review in context; localization is a re-edit. → [Localization](TEXT-ACCESSIBILITY/Localization.md), [Versioning and Localization](DELIVERY/Versioning-and-Localization.md)

## Sound

**Recording and cleanup.** Mic closer before software; room tone always; tie every process to an observed problem; compare at matched loudness; stop before robotic. → [Recording and Cleanup](AUDIO/Recording-and-Cleanup.md)

**Dialogue and voice.** First listen with notes; fill with room tone; cut at consonants; keep meaningful breaths; direct VO by intent; write for speech. → [Dialogue and Voice](AUDIO/Dialogue-and-Voice.md)

**Music selection.** Choose by function (trajectory, tension, timbre, density, room for dialogue); consider no music; avoid temp attachment; verify licence scope. → [Music Selection](AUDIO/Music-Selection.md)

**Music editing.** Edit on bars and phrases; keep pickups; manage tails; backtime endings; reserve exact sync for accents. → [Music Editing](AUDIO/Music-Editing.md)

**Sound design.** Purpose before effects; layer by role (onset, body, detail, space, tail); remove redundant layers before EQ; in documentary, never invent events. → [Sound Design](AUDIO/Sound-Design.md)

**Perspective and silence.** Match distance cues; dialogue clear even in wides; silence is designed — reduced sound or room tone, rarely digital zero. → [Perspective and Silence](AUDIO/Perspective-and-Silence.md)

**Mix.** Hierarchy follows story; arrange before you duck; dBFS ≠ dBTP ≠ LUFS; check mono, phones and low volume; mix for the destination's normalisation, not for loudness. → [Mix Strategy](AUDIO/Mix-Strategy.md)

**Audio delivery.** Use each destination's target and measurement method; measure the delivered file; deliver stems and M&E. → [Audio Delivery](DELIVERY/Audio-Delivery.md)

**Synthetic audio.** Brief by event, perspective, environment, variation and prohibitions; diagnose the failing property before regenerating; separate permission, consent and authenticity; full artefact and rights checklist before approval. → [Generation and Layering](AI-AUDIO/Generation-and-Layering.md), [Voice and Music QC](AI-AUDIO/Voice-and-Music-QC.md)

## Camera and light

**Composition.** First question: what must the audience notice, understand, feel, from whose perspective? Perspective comes from position, field of view from focal length and format; depth from placement before lenses; shoot for the edit and for every format. → [Composition and Optics](CAMERA/Composition-and-Optics.md)

**Capture.** Plan capture rate for retiming; shutter is a choice (180° is a reference); expose for important tones and judge clipping on the log signal; flicker-safe shutters; lock phone settings. → [Capture Decisions](CAMERA/Capture-Decisions.md)

**Movement.** Move to reveal, follow, approach, subjectify, reshape or scale — not to fill time; start and stop on stable frames; imperfection helps observation and hurts precision. → [Motivated Movement](CAMERA/Motivated-Movement.md)

**Retiming and virtual motion.** Real slow motion needs capture headroom; interpolation invents frames; ramps can erase action; a digital push is not a dolly; stills have no unseen geometry. → [Retiming and Virtual Motion](CAMERA/Retiming-and-Virtual-Motion.md)

**Light.** Read before lighting; softness is apparent size; falloff separates; negative fill models; CCT match ≠ spectral match; three functions, not three fixtures; the grade cannot invent real shadows. → [Reading and Designing Light](LIGHTING/Reading-and-Designing-Light.md), [Practical Setups](LIGHTING/Practical-Setups.md)

## Colour and finishing

**Colour management.** Confirm interpretation first; scene- vs display-referred; one output transform for all sources; a LUT is not a pipeline; beware double transforms and range mismatches; HDR needs HDR monitoring. → [Color Management](COLOR/Color-Management.md)

**Correction and matching.** Primaries in order; scopes diagnose, eyes decide; the skin line is an aid, not a target; preserve intentional lighting differences. → [Correction and Shot Matching](COLOR/Correction-and-Shot-Matching.md)

**Creative grading.** A look is palette, contrast structure, highlights, saturation distribution, separation and attention; invisible grades suit observation; respect skin and complexion; scope compliance is not taste. → [Creative Grading](COLOR/Creative-Grading.md)

**Texture and relighting.** Shape plausibly within recorded light; denoise early, grain late, sharpen at output; check after encoding. → [Texture and Post Relighting](COLOR/Texture-and-Post-Relighting.md)

**Compositing.** Is the effect needed? Practical first. Match space, light, optics and medium; mind premultiplication; plan plates and data on set; LED volumes are acquisition decisions. → [Compositing and Integration](VFX/Compositing-and-Integration.md)

**Repair.** Fix what viewers perceive; mask privacy robustly; generative removal can erase evidence; keep originals and a change log. → [Repair and Finishing](VFX/Repair-and-Finishing.md)

## AI workflows

**Assistance and control.** Classify the task; AI drafts, humans decide meaning, testimony, consent and truth; keep work reversible; don't upload without permission; brief with acceptance conditions; iterate on defects. → [Assistance and Control](AI-WORKFLOWS/Assistance-and-Control.md)

**Generated media.** Plausible is not true; inspect identity, hands, objects, text, light, physics, stereotypes and factual implication; previews are not deliverables. → [Generated Media QC](AI-WORKFLOWS/Generated-Media-QC.md)

## Delivery

**Platforms and formats.** Design for context (muted starts, interfaces, first frames); recompose and re-edit versions; verify current specs. → [Platform and Format Strategy](DELIVERY/Platform-and-Format-Strategy.md)

**Versions.** One matrix; parents recorded; names that prevent stale exports; series inherit specifications. → [Versioning and Localization](DELIVERY/Versioning-and-Localization.md)

**QC and export.** Profiles from current documents; masters vs distribution copies; staged QC including flash risk; encoded playback; signoff with evidence — an export finishing is not a check. → [Quality Control and Export](DELIVERY/Quality-Control-and-Export.md)

## Diagnosis and coherence

**Diagnosis.** Intent → story → performance → structure → picture → sound → text → finishing; isolate layers; re-watch the whole. → [Failure Modes and Repair](DIAGNOSTICS/Failure-Modes-and-Repair.md)

**Coherence.** Write the piece's rules; justify every exception; audit with evidence; stop when objectives are met, defects are gone and returns diminish. → [Creative Coherence and Taste](FOUNDATIONS/Creative-Coherence-and-Taste.md)
