# Start Here — World-Class Video Direction Knowledge System

**What this is:** a reusable masterclass and decision reference for directing and finishing video — from the creative brief and the interview through editing, motion design, sound, synthetic audio, captions, camera, lighting, colour, VFX, AI-assisted workflows and delivery — ending with how all of it becomes one coherent piece.

**What it is not:** a set of rules, a software manual, a legal guide, or an analysis of any real footage. Every example uses clearly labelled hypothetical projects.

---

## 1. Who it is for

| Reader | What you will get |
|---|---|
| Newcomers to post-production | The questions experts ask, the cues they inspect, and the reasons behind their choices — with worked examples you can follow |
| Working editors, designers, mixers, colorists, camera and lighting people | Decision frameworks across neighbouring disciplines, so you can anticipate how your choices affect others; failure/repair references |
| Directors and producers | Briefing, resource priorities, review handling, rights and delivery gates; a shared vocabulary with every department |
| Teachers | Structured chapters with examples and counterexamples; late-change exercises in the worked projects |

## 2. How to read it

1. **Read [Decision Method](FOUNDATIONS/Decision-Method.md) first** (15 minutes). It defines the decision pattern, the confidence labels used everywhere, the units for frames and levels, and the four recurring hypothetical projects.
2. **Choose a route** from the [Master Index](01-MASTER-INDEX.md#reading-routes): core, editor, sound, image, design and text, producer, or quick reference.
3. **Follow one worked project end to end** ([overview](WORKED-PROJECTS/00-Overview.md)) to see how decisions in one department create obligations in others.
4. **Use the [Master Checklist](MASTER-CHECKLIST.md)** on real projects, and the [Worksheets and Logs](TEMPLATES/Worksheets-and-Logs.md) as starting forms.
5. **Look up terms** in the [Glossary](GLOSSARY.md); each term points to the chapter that owns it.

## 3. How to read the claims

Every important claim carries a confidence label:

| Label | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `[Requirement]` | A verified external specification | Comply — and re-check the current version before claiming compliance |
| `[Practice]` | Established professional practice | Follow unless you have a reason not to |
| `[Heuristic]` | Context-dependent guideline | Test it against your material |
| `[Preference]` | Aesthetic choice | Decide by brief and coherence |
| `[Illustrative]` | An assumption for teaching | Never copy into a delivery spec |

What was and was not verified — and what remains uncertain — is listed in [Sources and Limitations](SOURCES-AND-LIMITATIONS.md).

## 4. Honest usage guidance

- **No real footage was analysed.** Observations about pictures, levels and colours are hypothetical and labelled.
- **Specifications change.** Loudness targets, caption limits, platform dimensions and AI product terms must be checked against the current destination document before delivery.
- **Not legal advice.** Rights, consent and disclosure chapters describe production practice; laws and contracts govern real obligations.
- **Taste is arguable.** The library explains criteria so disagreements can be resolved by evidence and intent, not by authority.
- **Language examples** (Hindi) are illustrative and should be reviewed by a fluent speaker before production reuse.

## 5. Three ways to use the library

| Mode | When | How |
|---|---|---|
| Full library (default) | Learning, team onboarding, reference | Follow routes; read worked projects |
| Project application | You have a real brief or footage | Copy the worked-project structure; fill the worksheets with real facts; run the master checklist; keep evidence labels honest ([Decision Method](FOUNDATIONS/Decision-Method.md#8-applying-this-library-to-a-real-project)) |
| Focused domain | You need depth in one area | Start at the domain's owner chapter in the [Master Index](01-MASTER-INDEX.md); follow its "Dependencies" links outward |
