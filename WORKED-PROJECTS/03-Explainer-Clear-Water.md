# Worked Project 3 — "Clear Water" (Bilingual Educational Explainer)

**Purpose:** Demonstrate an integrated educational workflow: factual clarity, demonstration evidence, information pacing, honest charts and labels, spoken explanation, readable captions in Hindi and English, an accessibility complication, and a late factual change across both language versions.

**Status:** hypothetical. Fixed assumptions are in [Decision Method](../FOUNDATIONS/Decision-Method.md#project-c--clear-water-educational-explainer). Lab data are invented for teaching. All times are illustrative.

**Frame rate and timecode:** 29.97 fps, non-drop-frame timecode (documented at ingest because music, captions and a screen recording share the clock; see [Ingest and Preparation](../OPERATIONS/Ingest-and-Preparation.md#12-worked-example-2--screen-recordings-and-music-for-project-c-hypothetical)). Times below are programme time (mm:ss).

## 1. Brief and assumptions

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Intent | After watching, a viewer can explain in their own words how the filter cleans water, list the four maintenance steps in order, and know when to boil water instead |
| Audience | Households with mixed literacy; many on low-cost phones with small speakers; Hindi and English speakers |
| Language | Hindi (primary audience) and English versions; closed captions in both; social cut-downs with open captions |
| Viewing context | Public-health channel website; a regional broadcaster (Hindi version); social; community health workers showing it on phones |
| Truths | Claims approved by the public-health advisor; no implication that the filter removes everything; real demonstration, not simulated |
| Emotional stance | A trustworthy neighbour showing you at a kitchen counter |
| Duration | About 5 minutes per language |
| Resources | One demonstration session (cannot be reshot); motion designer; two narrators; tight budget |
| Approvals | Advisor (facts, per language), channel producer (editorial), translator/reviewer (language) |
| Success indicators | Step recall and mechanism explanation in small viewer tests per language |

**Accessibility complication:** many viewers read Hindi slowly or not at all; captions cannot be the only carrier of essential instructions. **Response:** essential steps are *spoken* and *shown*, with captions and labels as support — not the sole channel.

**What would change the approach:** if the audience were mostly literate English-speaking urban viewers, more on-screen text and a faster pace could work; if the advisor approved only very cautious claims, the chart might be removed altogether in favour of demonstration.

## 4. Source-gathering strategy

There is no testimony; the "sources" are the advisor, the lab data and the demonstration.
- **Advisor sessions** before scripting: what may be claimed, what must be qualified, which terms households use.
- **Glossary** in both languages (e.g., candle → "कैंडल"; see [Localization](../TEXT-ACCESSIBILITY/Localization.md#terminology-example-hypothetical-project-c)).
- **Demonstration planning:** a shot list derived from steps, with each step shown from an angle where hands and object are both visible; a "mistake" shot (soap) staged clearly as a demonstration of what not to do.
- **Sensitive areas:** health claims; anything implying that a filter makes water universally safe.

## 5. Selects and sequence map

| Sequence | Selects | Criterion |

## 7. Pacing, attention, geography and camera

- **Pacing:** comprehension pauses after each step (about one breath of narration silence plus a hold on the result); the history segment cut from 40 s to 12 s (see [Rhythm and Pacing](../EDITING/Rhythm-and-Pacing.md#11-counterexample-and-repair--trimming-every-pause)).
- **Attention:** one thing moves at a time; labels appear after parts settle; captions moved to the top during demonstrations so hands remain visible (see [Captions and Accessibility](../TEXT-ACCESSIBILITY/Captions-and-Accessibility.md#12-worked-example-2--dense-tutorial-labels-hypothetical-project-c-clear-water-2997-fps-ndf)).
- **Geography:** the counter is the only space; consistent left-to-right placement of filter, brush and bowl across angles.
- **Camera:** locked-off; no moves during demonstrations (stability for learning).

## 8. Lighting and acquisition

- Soft window-motivated key from the side; white bounce for gentle fill; the counter kept free of glare (reflections on the water surface controlled with flags).
- Exposure placed so water clarity differences are visible; white balance neutral so "cloudy" vs "clear" is judged honestly.
- Limits: the wide shot of steps 2 and 3 is soft at the edges — addressed with animated inserts rather than digital punch-ins that would lose clarity.

## 9. Colour pipeline, look and repair limits

- Simple managed workflow; SDR BT.709 output; legal range; consistent tags.
- **Look:** neutral, clean, warm enough to feel domestic; water colour rendered faithfully — no grading that makes dirty water look dirtier or clean water bluer than it is (factual representation).
- **Charts and diagrams:** colours chosen for colour-vision accessibility; brand colours checked on output.
- **Repair limits:** the soft wide shot cannot be sharpened into clarity; the inserts carry the detail.

## 14. Review notes, diagnosis, repair and acceptance

The advisor/client round is in [Review and Revisions](../OPERATIONS/Review-and-Revisions.md#8-worked-example-2--advisor-and-client-review-of-an-explainer-hypothetical-project-c): unsupported 99% claim removed; "tighter" diagnosed as the history section; Hindi captions re-chunked in step 3; "add a boiling section" priced and reduced to a 6-second safety line.

**Final acceptance criteria:** (1) per language, at least four of five test viewers list the steps in order and explain the mechanism; (2) advisor approves every claim in both languages; (3) captions accurate and readable on low-cost phones; (4) no viewer identifies it as an advertisement; (5) QC passed per version.

## 16. Late change — revised factual wording after picture lock

**Change:** the advisor revises the line "removes 99% of bacteria" to "reduces most bacteria when the candle is maintained; it does not remove viruses — boil water if unsure." This happens after picture lock, in both languages.

The full change-impact map is in [Constraints and Priorities](../FOUNDATIONS/Constraints-and-Priorities.md#change-impact-map--filled-example-hypothetical-project-c-clear-water). Project-specific consequences:

| Area | Hindi version | English version |
|---|---|---|
| Story | The limits beat gains importance; it moves from 04:35 to directly after the chart, so the qualification follows the evidence | Same structure change |
| Narration | Two lines re-recorded; Hindi lines run ≈3 s longer than the old line | Two lines re-recorded; ≈2 s longer |
| Cuts | Demonstration shot under the line extended from handles; boiling cutaway inserted from existing B-roll | Same, shorter extension |
| Music | Extension falls mid-phrase; one bar of pad repeated from the stem | Same; check ending lands |
| Chart | Annotation changed to "much lower when cleaned weekly"; animated counter removed | Same |
| Captions | New cues; reading rate checked; caption file version incremented | Same |
| Typography | Hindi line breaks for the new safety line tested for clipping of matras | English line length checked |
| Mix | New VO lines matched; loudness re-measured per destination | Same |
| Grade | Boiling cutaway graded to match | Same |
| Versions | Social 60 s cuts flagged and checked (they include the chart) | Same |
| Broadcast | Revised Hindi master re-submitted to broadcaster QC | — |
| Approvals | Advisor approves exact wording in each language; channel producer signs | Same |

## 17. Integrated result

**What was sacrificed and why:**
- **A dramatic opening** — sacrificed for trust; the educational contract requires calm accuracy.
- **A headline statistic** — sacrificed for honesty; the qualified claim is weaker but true.
- **Visual polish** (glossy macros, slow motion) — sacrificed for an ordinary kitchen that viewers recognise.
- **Speed** — sacrificed for comprehension pauses.

**Disconfirming test:** if, after watching, fewer than three of five viewers in either language can list the steps, or if viewers believe the filter makes any water safe to drink, the approach has failed. Repair would start with the limits beat's placement and wording and the step pauses — not with adding animation.
