# Worked Project 2 — "Arc" (Product Spot, Brand Film and Vertical Versions)

**Purpose:** Demonstrate an integrated advertising workflow: a clear audience and action, a legible product reveal, considered camera and motion grammar, music and sound emphasis, graphic hierarchy, horizontal and vertical adaptation, and a late duration change — with every department's decisions traced to the brief.

**Status:** hypothetical. Fixed assumptions are in [Decision Method](../FOUNDATIONS/Decision-Method.md#project-b--arc-product-and-brand-campaign). All times are illustrative.

**Frame rate and timecode:** 23.976 fps (non-drop-frame timecode); times below are given in seconds of programme time.

---

## 1. Brief and assumptions

The filled brief is in [Creative Brief](../FOUNDATIONS/Creative-Brief.md#filled-example--project-b-arc-30-second-spot-hypothetical). Key points:

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Intent (30 s spot) | A muted phone viewer who already owns a bottle can say what is different about Arc (it shows hydration progress at a glance) and perceives it as precisely made, not gadget-like |
| Intent (90 s brand film) | Viewers feel Arc comes from a real person's concern |
| Audience | Urban professionals 25–45 with reusable bottles; phone-first; often muted at first |
| Truths | The ring shows progress toward a target set in the app; it does not measure water quality; no health claims |
| Stance | Calm confidence; a quiet companion |
| Deliverables | 90 s brand film (16:9), 30 s spot (16:9), 15 s vertical (9:16), 6 s bumper, M&E/textless |

**What would change the approach:** if the media plan moved to sound-on placements, voice could carry the claim and on-screen text could shrink; if legal rejected the progress claim wording, the reveal would need a different proof (app-first); if the audience shifted to fitness enthusiasts, the brand stance itself would need revisiting — not just faster cutting.

## 3. Story and beat maps

### 30-second spot (compressed demonstration with montage opening)

| Beat | Viewer knows | Feels |
|---|---|---|
| Morning rush | "This is my habit" | Recognition |
| Hesitation over the bottle | Something is about to happen | Anticipation |
| Ring lights as she drinks | The bottle shows progress | Small surprise |
| Claim and app | It's personal and connected | Understanding |
| End card | Name, benefit, where to buy | Calm confidence |

### 90-second brand film (narrative arc)

Causal chain from [Story Architecture](../STORY/Story-Architecture.md#10-rough-cut-diagnostic-for-beautiful-but-shapeless-material): *Dev's father became ill → Dev noticed how hard it is to notice your own habits → so Dev built something that notices for you → but the first prototypes were ugly and annoying → so Dev made it quiet → therefore Arc feels like a companion, not a coach.*

## 4. Testimony strategy (brand film)

From [Interview Craft](../STORY/Interview-Craft.md#11-worked-example-1--founder-interview-for-a-brand-film-hypothetical-project-b-arc): workshop setting; prototype-failure warm-up; follow-ups that turn pitch language into specific scenes; patient silence that produced the father story; legal off-limits (health claims, competitors); Dev reviews the story section.

## 5. Selects and sequence map

**Brand film assembly:** the founder lines were ordered on the transcript into the causal chain before any timeline work and built as a radio cut (see the [filled paper edit](../OPERATIONS/Paper-Edit-and-Assembly.md#filled-paper-edit--project-b-arc-brand-film-hypothetical-23976-fps)). The 30-second spot needs no paper edit: it has no testimony and its assembly plan is the music-led structure in Section 15.

| Sequence (30 s) | Key selects | Performance criterion |
|---|---|---|
| Montage | Alarm, keys, laptop, train, desk | Specific actions, not generic "busy" shots |
| Hesitation | Hand hovering (take with the most natural pause) | A believable hesitation, not a mime |
| Reveal | Slider push-in macro; ring lights as the move settles | Smooth settle; ring in focus |
| Drink | Real-time hand-and-drink shot; 20% slow insert of the stream | Natural drinking, not posed |
| App | Phone UI replacement shot | Hand relaxed; glance natural |
| End card | Graphic | — |

## 7. Pacing, attention, geography and camera

- **Pacing:** moderate montage density → drop to near-silence for the hover → chorus release (see [Music Editing](../AUDIO/Music-Editing.md#4-worked-example-1--phrase-level-rearrangement-hypothetical-project-b-arc-30-s-23976-fps)).
- **Attention:** in the reveal, the darkest area is the lid region except for the ring; the eye lands on the glow.
- **Camera:** slow slider push-in settling before the event (see [Motivated Movement](../CAMERA/Motivated-Movement.md#7-worked-example-1--a-controlled-product-reveal-hypothetical-project-b-arc-23976-fps)); locked-off for the bumper.
- **Geography:** minimal — a kitchen and a desk; continuity of the bottle's position and water level tracked.

## 8. Lighting and acquisition

- Reflective product: strip sources for gradient highlights; black flags for edges; lid area kept dark so the ring reads (see [Practical Setups](../LIGHTING/Practical-Setups.md#5-reflective-product-hypothetical-project-b-arc)).
- HFR pour with flicker-free fixtures and more light (see [Capture Decisions](../CAMERA/Capture-Decisions.md#10-worked-example-2--high-frame-rate-product-pour-hypothetical-project-b-23976-fps-timeline)).
- Vertical framings shot for key moments (see [Composition and Optics](../CAMERA/Composition-and-Optics.md#8-worked-example-2--a-product-demonstration-hypothetical-project-b-arc)).

## 9. Colour pipeline, look and repair limits

- **Pipeline:** scene-referred wide-gamut working space; per-camera input transforms; CG UI in the compositing working space; SDR outputs; HDR only if a destination spec confirms it (see [Color Management](../COLOR/Color-Management.md#5-a-complex-mixed-camera-workflow-hypothetical-project-b-arc)).
- **Look:** warm-neutral; saturation reserved for the ring; montage slightly cooler (see [Creative Grading](../COLOR/Creative-Grading.md#7-worked-example-2--a-stylised-brand-scene-hypothetical-project-b-arc-30-second-spot)).
- **Repair limits:** ring colour clipping in one channel cannot be recovered if clipped at capture — tested on set; fingerprints are removed by cleaning on set, not in post (post cleanup reserved for rare misses).
- **Skin:** founder rendered naturally; no beauty smoothing (see [Texture and Post Relighting](../COLOR/Texture-and-Post-Relighting.md#9-counterexample-and-repair--the-beauty-pass-that-removed-a-person)).

## 14. Review notes, diagnosis, repair and acceptance

The round-2 review log is in [Review and Revisions](../OPERATIONS/Review-and-Revisions.md#filled-review-log-hypothetical-project-b-arc-30-s-round-2-23976-fps): "slow start" → repetitive shots removed; "ring too fast to notice" → hover extended; legal claim correction; caption covering ring in 9:16 → moved; "make the logo bigger" → longer hold and contrast within guidelines.

**Hinge variable resolved:** legal approved the progress claim wording but declined the "up to 3 weeks" battery-life claim pending independent testing (it was flagged as open in the brief). As planned, the spot carries no battery claim; the app glance serves as the second proof point.

**Final acceptance criteria:** (1) muted fresh viewers state the benefit; (2) descriptions use words like "calm", "clean", "smart"; (3) legal approves all claims and UI; (4) brand lead approves colour and logo; (5) QC passed for every version.

## 16. Late change — the spot must become 20 seconds

**Change:** after approval of the 30 s spot, the media buyer secures a placement that accepts only 20-second spots. The brand lead asks for a 20 s version within two days.

**Intent check:** the 20 s version must still let a muted viewer state the benefit and perceive calm precision.

### Change-impact map

| Area | Effect | Action | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Story | Montage cannot carry four shots; app beat may not fit | Keep: alarm → one montage shot → hesitation → ring → drink + claim → end card. Drop: train, laptop, app glance | Fresh viewers state the benefit muted |
| Cuts | New structure at 10 bars (20 s) | Recut from approved selects | Full-speed review |
| Music phrases | 15 bars → 10 bars: intro 1, montage 2, build 1, hover 1, chorus 3, ending 2 | Re-edit with stems; joins at matching harmony; natural ending preserved by backtiming | Musician-ear check |
| Pacing | Hover must survive (it is the proof moment) | Keep one full bar of hover | Ring noticed |
| Claim text | Hold reduced from ≈5 s to ≈3.5 s | Reading check on phones; wording unchanged (no legal re-approval needed) | Read-aloud test |
| VO and captions | End-card VO still fits 4 s ending | Caption re-timed | Encoded check |
| Graphics | End card unchanged; claim timing changed | Motion designer re-times | Static and motion review |
| Grade | No new shots | — | — |
| VFX handoff | App UI shot dropped from this version only | Inform VFX lead; no re-render | Version matrix note |
| Sound/mix | New music edit; Foley for dropped shots removed | Mix updated; loudness and true peak re-measured | Readings recorded |
| Versions | New row ARC-SP20-169-EN; parent ARC-SP30-169-EN v07; vertical 15 s still derives from the 30 s | Matrix updated | Reconciled |

## 17. Integrated result

**What was sacrificed and why:**
- **Spectacle** (fast orbit, speed ramps, bouncy graphics) — sacrificed for the calm-confidence stance.
- **Beat-perfect sync everywhere** — sacrificed for action-led cutting with a few accents, so the reveal is the moment that stands out.
- **Founder presence in the spot** — left to the brand film, keeping the spot focused on the proof.
- **App beat in the 20 s version** — sacrificed to protect the ring reveal.

**Disconfirming test:** if, in a muted phone test, viewers of the 30 s and 20 s spots cannot state the ring's function, or describe Arc as "a fitness gadget", the approach has failed — the reveal is not legible or the stance is not landing. Repair would begin with shot scale and timing of the ring (Section 6), not with more music or effects.
